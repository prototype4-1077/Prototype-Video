# The Twelve-Dollar Mug — opening porch motion study

Play `porch-life-review.mp4` at 1×. Original on the left, local
movement on the right. During the 4.3-second opening, June's
upper body settles, his head tilts slightly toward the mug,
and one gust nudges the chime's lower paddle. The roof hook,
mug and grasp, chair, boots, and porch stay planted. This
tests whether quiet motion makes the opening feel less like
a still without distracting from the borrowed-mug setup.

than the wall and restores only the exposed boundary.
