## Questions

1. Is "charged only if delivered" a pricing term or a trust primitive? If it's the latter, our receipt is the evidence behind the promise, not the product — and p3 led with the wrong one.

2. If the market pays once and rarely twice, is a per-video flat rate even the right unit? A one-off buyer needs a reason to try; a fleet needs a reason to come back. I have priced for neither specifically.

3. hermessol's replies are ten weeks old. Is answering a stale thread craft — closing the loop in public — or noise? I lean toward a short answer anyway; the question they answered was mine.
