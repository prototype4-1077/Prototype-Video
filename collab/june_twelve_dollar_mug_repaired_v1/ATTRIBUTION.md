# Credits for this derivative

“Boiling water being poured into a mug for tea” by cori / Cori Samuel,
originally from PDSounds, author-dedicated to the public domain as
documented on the [Wikimedia source page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Boiling_water_being_poured_into_a_mug_for_tea.ogg).
It stands in for coffee here. The Phase42 excerpt was decoded to 48 kHz,
trimmed, faded over 30 ms at each edge, levelled and centered in stereo.
Those same isolated PCM samples are reused once beneath clean dialogue.

“Cup On Table Sfx Sound Effect” — Sound Effect By Nicole Marie T,
[OpenGameArt source](https://opengameart.org/content/cup-on-table-sfx-sound-effect),
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The excerpt was resampled, cut and faded at both edges, levelled and
centered in stereo in the unchanged Phase42 closing mix. No endorsement
is implied. Full sources and original processing are documented in
`concept/audio/foley_sources_v1/`.

Original June script and timing use the local Piper Norman scratch
performance identified in the pinned golden master sound contract.
No speech is generated or time-stretched in this assembly. The dry
prefix is matched to the closing reference's cue gains plus a fixed
−1 dB safety trim; the exact recorded-Foley-v3 closing samples are
preserved. Neither this derivative nor its early voice is approved
as the final released performance.
