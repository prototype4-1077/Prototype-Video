# Recorded Foley sources — v1

These two existing recordings were downloaded without payment or an account
on 2026-10-28. No inference API or stock subscription was used. Their raw
downloads are preserved here; `sources.json` records exact bytes, hashes,
excerpt ranges, mix levels, and source/licensing pages.

## Pour

“Boiling water being poured into a mug for tea” by cori / Cori Samuel,
originally from PDSounds. The [Wikimedia Commons source page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Boiling_water_being_poured_into_a_mug_for_tea.ogg)
states that the author released this recording into the public domain.
The downloaded file's SHA-1 matches that page's original-file metadata.
It records kettle water into a tea mug, used here as a coffee-pour stand-in.

## Cup contact

Sound Effect By Nicole Marie T — “Cup On Table Sfx Sound Effect,” from
[OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/cup-on-table-sfx-sound-effect),
licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Changes and downstream credits

Both are decoded/resampled locally to 48-kHz mono, trimmed to the sample
ranges in `sources.json`, faded over 30 ms at both excerpt edges, levelled
and placed in stereo. No looping, pitch shifting, synthesized tones, or
voice modification is used. These are adaptations, not the unmodified
recordings. No endorsement by either author is implied.

Carry this attribution and the source/license links with any audio or video
that uses these excerpts. The audition is not human-accepted and does not
establish that these effects suit the final character, scene, or mix.
